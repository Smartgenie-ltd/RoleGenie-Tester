RoleGenie board

How to decide

Choose one decision per job, then tap Save. To change it later, tap another and Save.

Apply
You would send an application for this job. A reason is required. RoleGenie does not apply for you: the daily run then tries to draft a tailored CV from your approved master CV only, and the card shows it as a draft when it is ready, or as held with the reason. Check it, apply yourself, then tap Already applied (or run python rolegenie.py mark <job> applied).
Consider
Not sure yet. A reason is optional. The job stays on your board, below the jobs you have not decided, and no CV is drafted. Decide when you are ready.
Reject
You would not apply even with a perfect CV. A reason is required, for example location, pay, seniority, not the work you want, or a hard requirement you cannot meet.
Already applied
You applied, through RoleGenie's draft or on your own. A reason is optional. The daily run records it as your application; only applications you mark this way are matched to recruiter replies.

No decision: The job stays at the top of your board and nothing is drafted.

Reading a card: Unmet hard requirements are always listed, whatever the score. A low score is not an automatic Reject: a stretch you can argue for is a fair Apply.

Your reasons: Words in your Apply, Reject and Already applied reasons decide which new jobs RoleGenie assesses first. For this, only your latest decision on each job counts, and Consider teaches nothing. Your decisions and ratings never change the fit score on a card.
